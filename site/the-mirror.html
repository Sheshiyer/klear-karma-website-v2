Mirror Listens Before It Suggests

Mirror is a reflective guide—not a chatbot, not an algorithm, not a quiz. It asks one question at a time, reflects back what it heard, and waits for you to say: "Yes, that's it."

Only then does it suggest a path forward.

One Question at a Time

Mirror doesn't overwhelm you with forms. Each question builds on the last.

Reflect and Confirm

After each answer, Mirror summarizes what it understood—you correct or confirm.

Your Context, Your Consent

Nothing is shared with practitioners until you explicitly say so. You control what goes forward.
